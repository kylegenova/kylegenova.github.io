About

I'm a Staff Research Scientist at Google DeepMind on Thomas Funkhouser's team, where I work on pretraining for the 3D understanding and generation capabilities of frontier models. I've contributed to the pretraining of Gemini 3 and Vision Banana, as well as next-generation models in the Gemini, Gemini Omni, Nano Banana, and Genie families. My research background is 3D computer vision, including 3D shape representation and scene understanding.

I received my Ph.D. from Princeton University, advised by Thomas Funkhouser in the Vision & Robotics Group. Before that, I studied Computer Science at Cornell University, where I worked with David Williamson on theoretical computer science and combinatorial optimization.
