About Me

I'm a Research Scientist at Google DeepMind, on Thomas Funkhouser's team in the Foundational Research Unit led by David Salesin and Rahul Sukthankar. My Ph.D. was on 3D computer vision, including 3D shape representation and scene understanding tasks. Currently I'm mostly working on Gemini pretraining projects related to 3D understanding.

Previously, I was a Ph.D. student at Princeton University, where I was also advised by Thomas Funkhouser in the Vision & Robotics Group. Before that, I was an undergraduate in Computer Science at Cornell University, where I worked with David Williamson in the areas of theoretical computer science and combinatorial optimization.
